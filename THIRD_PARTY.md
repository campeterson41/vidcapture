# Third-party components

Interface SVG icons are adapted from Tabler Icons by Paweł Kuna, MIT: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons . License: licenses/tabler-icons-MIT.txt.

The circular webcam technique was informed by https://github.com/SomeoneWithOptions/loom-omarchy-linux . Its MIT notice is preserved in licenses/loom-omarchy-linux-MIT.txt.

The interface was developed with AI assistance. No paid design product or upstream application bundle is included.

Window following builds GPU Screen Recorder 6.1.4 from its pinned upstream source with the included GPL-3.0-only patch. Source revision, patch, upstream link, and reproduction steps are in engine/. Full license: licenses/gpu-screen-recorder-GPL-3.0.txt. No precompiled helper is distributed in this source release.

The browser video card preview uses gifenc 1.0.3. Its license is in cloud/public/gifenc-LICENSE.md. VidCapture invokes system Electron, GPU Screen Recorder, MPV, FFmpeg, PulseAudio utilities, Omarchy, Hyprland, and v4l2 utilities. Those retain their own licenses and are installed separately.
